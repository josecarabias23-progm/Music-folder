# Proposal: Groups, Shared Library & Community

## Summary
Add a new feature in Music Folder for group-based collaboration with a director-led model. The feature includes group creation, member management, a shared music library, rehearsal planning, and communication spaces for both internal group interaction and broader community discussion.

## What & Why

### Problem
The platform currently supports personal score management and general forum discussions, but it lacks a dedicated environment where a director can manage a music group, coordinate students, and centralize all rehearsals, materials, and communication.

### Solution
Introduce a group module with:
- director-managed group lifecycle
- unique group join code assigned to the director
- student entry flow using the group code to join the group
- members and roles
- shared library for scores and materials
- rehearsal sessions and attendance tracking
- internal communication and announcements
- community space for broader conversations and learning

## Goals
1. Director creates and manages a group.
2. Members can join and access relevant content.
3. Shared music library for scores and rehearsal resources.
4. Group rehearsal planning and attendance visibility.
5. Community and announcement channels for active communication.

## User Stories

### Director
- I want to create a group for my ensemble or school.
- I want a unique join code assigned to my group so students can join easily.
- I want to invite students and track who belongs to the group.
- I want to share a library of repertoire and references.
- I want to schedule rehearsals and keep notes and attendance.
- I want to communicate with everyone through announcements and discussions.

### Member / Student
- I want to enter the group code given by the director to join the group.
- I want to access the group library for practice.
- I want to see rehearsals and join with correct information.
- I want to communicate with the director and other members.
- I want to ask questions or share ideas in the community.

## Acceptance Criteria
- The director can create a group with a name, description, visibility, and a unique join code.
- Students can join the group by entering the valid code assigned to the director.
- Members can join or be invited to the group.
- Shared files are visible only to group members according to access rules.
- Rehearsals are attached to the group and available to participants.
- Group announcements and discussions are kept separate from general public community posts.

## Non-Goals
- Direct messaging outside of group context.
- Video playback or live remote sessions in MVP.
- Payment or premium subscriptions.
- Massive social feed beyond group/community flows.
